# cr11-4: alert and retention custody pauses lack biting guards

**Severity**: LOW — current composition supplies the health dependency, but
the two destructive background mutation gates were optional and unproved.
**Status**: Verified
**Branch**: —
**Commit**: `17a8524f798788b3adf374b34ef0b98246991221`

## Evidence

`RetentionService` and `AlertEvaluationService` accepted a nullable optional
`CustodyHealthState`, treating omission as permission to mutate. The suite
proved ingest refusal only; it never made custody unhealthy and asserted that
retention and alert evaluation remain idle.

## Predicted observable failure

A future construction-site omission or guard regression can delete retained
evidence or append alert custody state on a branch the witness has already
declared unhealthy or restore-pending.

## What

Two S4b item-5 mutation gates lacked the item-7 biting proof and were easier
to omit than the composition contract intended.

## Approach

Make `CustodyHealthState` a required non-null constructor dependency for both
services. Extract one alert-evaluation iteration as an internal seam and add a
single test that drives both services unhealthy, observes no mutation, then
restores health and proves the queued work and retention sweep proceed.

## Guard proof

`RetentionEnforcementTests::Unhealthy_custody_pauses_retention_and_alert_evaluation`
proves both mutation classes remain idle while unhealthy and resume after a
healthy transition. Removing the retention guard made the test fail because
`SweepOnceAsync` returned a retention outcome; restoring it and independently
removing the alert guard made the test fail because alert evaluation returned
true. Restoring both guards passes.

## Coder dispute (if any)

failure; the candidate is admitted for the explicit missing S4b mutation
guards and the nullable dependency that made regression silent.
