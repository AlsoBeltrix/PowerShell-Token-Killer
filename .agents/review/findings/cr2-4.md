# cr2-4: Quarantine actions never enter the audit journal

Severity: MEDIUM. Source: codereview codex (gpt-5.6-sol @ high, standard)
over `61fc838..a10ad50`, 2026-08-10. Status: fixed, verification pending.

## Evidence

`server/PtkMcpServer/Audit/AuditJournalFactory.cs` — the quarantine path
moved `host.id` and wrote stderr only; no journal record of the tampering
or the recovery existed, violating the plan's rule-3 reporting contract
(surface (a): the quarantine is itself a first-class audit event).

## Predicted observable failure

After a corrupt `host.id` is quarantined, the JSONL journal contains no
record the incident occurred; with stderr unretained, monitoring and later
export cannot alert on it or correlate the fresh identity with the
quarantined artifact.

## What

The factory parks a bounded detail code
(`quarantine.host_identity`) on the constructed journal
(`RecordPendingStartupQuarantine`); `AuditServerLifecycle.EnsureStarted`
appends a durable `audit.quarantine` record (outcome `quarantined`,
`detail_code` set, parented to `server.started`) via the emergency
`AppendAutomaticTransition` lane before the lifecycle claims Started — a
failure to record it fails startup like any lifecycle append. Event and
detail codes are open machine codes in the frozen `ptk.audit/2` schema;
no schema change.

## Guard proof

`A_corrupt_host_identity_is_quarantined_and_service_continues` extended:
after shutdown the journal must contain `"audit.quarantine"` and
`"quarantine.host_identity"`. Stash-revert of the lifecycle change fails
exactly this assertion; restored, the full battery is 1,220/1,220.
