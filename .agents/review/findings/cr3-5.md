# cr3-5: Transient HTTP 408 responses permanently skip audit batches

Severity: MEDIUM. Source: codereview codex (gpt-5.6-sol @ high,
standard) over `1a7a71f..98a3625`, 2026-08-10. Status: CLOSED (fixed,
both halves).

## Evidence

`HttpAuditDestination` classified only 429/5xx/401/403 as retryable, so
408 fell through to Permanent, and `AuditExportService` advanced the
cursor past a Permanent batch of up to 256 records.

## Predicted observable failure

One transient 408 from a collector or proxy permanently skips up to 256
audit records instead of retrying them.

## What

Two halves. Classification: 408 and 425 join 429/5xx/401/403 as
retryable. Isolation: a permanently refused batch is re-delivered one
record at a time, so a genuine refusal costs exactly the refused record
instead of everything travelling with it; a retryable answer during
isolation aborts the pass and leaves the cursor for a clean retry.

## Guard proof

`A_transient_408_is_retried_instead_of_skipping_the_batch` and
`A_refused_batch_isolates_the_poison_record_and_keeps_the_rest` (a fake
destination that refuses only the poison body; both healthy records must
still be delivered). Two sabotages — 408 back to permanent, and
whole-batch skip — each fail exactly their test.
