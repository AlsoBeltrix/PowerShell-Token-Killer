# cr4-3: Concurrent supervisors can overwrite one another's boot lineage

**Severity**: HIGH — concurrent supervisors on one audit root are a
supported, common deployment (one per MCP connection), and the overwrite
defeats the range's primary loss-detection guarantee.
**Status**: Open
**Branch**: —
**Commit**: (pending)

Reviewer: codex / gpt-5.6-sol / high / standard (generation pass over
`abc3292..8ab189a`, 2026-08-11).

## Evidence

`AuditJournalFactory.OpenCore` reads the predecessor at journal OPEN
(AuditJournalFactory.cs:55 area) and `AuditBootLineage.TryPublish`
last-writer-wins the single lineage file (AuditBootLineage.cs:94). Two
supervisors A and B that open before either first-appends both capture
predecessor P; their later first-appends independently overwrite the
file, so neither attests the other, and both their records claim P.

## Predicted observable failure

If A's spool vanishes before delivery while B survives, B truthfully
appears to follow P and the exporter reports no unverified boundary for
A — an entire supervisor boot silently lost, the exact cr3-2 open
finding this feature closed for the serial case. Additionally, after
A delivers, B's claim of P raises a FALSE unverified boundary.

## What

Lineage resolution at open plus unsynchronized publish models boots as
serial; concurrent boots break both the attestation chain and the
false-positive guarantees.

## Approach

The predecessor is resolved and published ATOMICALLY at first append,
under the sink's existing cross-process spool quota lock: inside one
critical section the journal reads the current lineage head, serializes
its first record claiming exactly that head, appends it durably, and
publishes itself as the new head. Overlapping boots therefore chain
P → A → B in true first-append order regardless of open order, and each
boot is attested by its successor. Records after the first reuse the
memoized predecessor (lineage is a boot-level fact).

## Files changed

- `server/PtkMcpServer/Audit/AuditJournal.cs` — lazy resolve at first append
- `server/PtkMcpServer/Audit/FileAuditJournalSink.cs` — quota-locked exchange hook
- `server/PtkMcpServer/Audit/AuditBootLineage.cs` — exchange primitive
- `server/PtkMcpServer/Audit/AuditJournalFactory.cs` — stops resolving at open

## Guard proof

`AuditBootLineageTests.Concurrently_opened_boots_chain_in_first_append_order`
— opens two journals over one root BEFORE either appends, appends to
them in a chosen order, and asserts the second-appender's records claim
the FIRST appender's boot id (not the shared grandparent). Reverting the
fix makes this FAIL (both claim the grandparent); restoring makes it
PASS.
