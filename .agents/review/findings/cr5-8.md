# cr5-8: Webhook delivery failure is invisible in every health surface

**Severity**: MEDIUM — the entire paging surface can fail silently forever; operators cannot distinguish a healthy alert path from one that has never delivered.
**Status**: In progress (fix landed, verification pending)
**Branch**: —

## Evidence
`server/PtkMcpServer/Audit/Web/AuditAlertWebhookService.cs:199-204` —
non-success responses and exceptions simply `return false` without
updating any health object, stderr, or the journal. Intake note
strengthening the finding: the class's own doc comment (lines 16-17)
claims a dead webhook "is visible in the export health line it
decorates" — no such visibility exists anywhere.

## Predicted observable failure
A permanently unreachable or rejecting webhook retries forever while
`/api/health` and `ptk_state` show no alert-delivery problem.

## What
Alert delivery has no health accounting at all: no failure counter, no
last-failure class, no last-success timestamp, on any surface.

## Approach
`AuditExportHealthSnapshot` gained four trailing-optional webhook fields
(configured, consecutive failures, last failure class, last success
instant — nonsecret only, no URL). The webhook service records every
outcome (`http_<status>` or exception type on failure; a delivery stamp
on success) through new `AuditExportHealth` methods. The export status
line — the very line the class doc claimed decorated — now appends
`alert_webhook=ok` or `ALERT_WEBHOOK_FAILING=<n> detail=<code>`
(including on the export-not-configured line, since a webhook can exist
without an export destination), which reaches `ptk_state` for free; the
UI's `/api/health` carries a nested `alert_webhook` object. Nothing
couples to admission: these are snapshot fields on the reporting path
only.
