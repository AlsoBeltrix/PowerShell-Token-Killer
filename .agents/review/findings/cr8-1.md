## Evidence
`siem/PtkSiemReceiver/Storage/SqliteIngestStore.cs` — active-gap
handling runs only on the `chain_gap` branch; the normal commit path
inserts and advances without consulting `ReadActiveGap`.

## Predicted observable failure
seq1 commits, seq3 opens a gap, late seq2 chains normally (head=2),
seq3 replay chains normally (head=3): resumed without disposition;
gap stays open forever; `ix_gaps_active_boot` blocks future gaps.

## What
The healed-hole case was never wired into the state machine.

## Approach
Hash-proven arrival heals: the normal path still advances on verified
continuity (the record proves itself against the head — no authority
question), and after each such advance, while a gap is active, the
head absorbs any stored post-gap records that now chain contiguously;
when the head reaches or passes the gap's claimed sequence the gap is
marked `resumed` (custody `gap:resumed`, transition `healed`).
Operator disposition remains the sole authority for accepting LOSS —
records that never arrive. Design note recorded here deliberately:
the reviewer's suggestion (freeze the head even for verified records)
would let ordinary late delivery park a healthy chain behind an
operator action; verification is stronger evidence than a
disposition, so healing is automatic, and only loss needs a human.
