# cr8-2: Retention can erase queued subjects before evaluation, permanently suppressing alerts

**Severity**: HIGH — a committed work item's subject row (event or quarantine attempt) can be retention-deleted before evaluation; the rule then finds nothing, the cursor advances, and the alert is lost despite the durable enqueue.

## Evidence
`SqliteIngestStore.cs` — `EvaluateRule` treats a vanished subject as
non-matching while the cursor still advances; none of the four
retention delete queries exclude subjects of queue items beyond
`alert_cursor`.

## Predicted observable failure
With a backlog (crash, slow webhook), a sweep deletes the matching
event/quarantine row; evaluation yields no alert and commits past it.

## What
The exactly-once guarantee assumed the subject outlives the queue row.

## Approach
Retention never deletes a row still referenced by an unevaluated work
item: all four delete queries exclude subjects of `alert_queue` items
above the cursor (events by `event_id`, quarantine by `attempt_id`).
