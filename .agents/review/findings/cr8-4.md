## Evidence
`SqliteIngestStore.cs` — the opening attempt id is computed and
dropped; gaps persist no link to it; both quarantine delete queries
delete unconditionally.

## Predicted observable failure
Age/size sweep removes the opening attempt of an open gap.

## What
The gap row points at nothing durable.

## Approach
Schema v4: `gaps.opening_attempt_id`; `OpenGap` records it; both
quarantine deletes exclude attempts referenced by non-resumed gaps.
