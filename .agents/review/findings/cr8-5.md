# cr8-5: Processed queue rows and closed alerts have no reclamation path

**Severity**: MEDIUM — alert_queue grows one row per ingest forever and closed alerts accumulate; the store can exceed and outgrow its configured size bound toward disk exhaustion.

## Evidence
`SqliteIngestStore.cs` — evaluation only advances the cursor; no
retention path touches alert_queue or alerts.

## Predicted observable failure
Unbounded growth despite maximumTotalBytes.

## What
Two append-only tables that were never append-only by design.

## Approach
Evaluated queue rows are deleted atomically with the cursor advance
(the cursor is the durable memory; the row is spent). Closed alerts
age out in the retention sweep with one custody-chained tombstone
entry per sweep recording the deleted ids — the plan's rule: alert
deletion is retention-driven and itself custody-recorded. Open and
acknowledged alerts are never swept.
