# i30-1: verbose, information, and Write-Host records vanish entirely from completed calls

**Severity**: HIGH (raised from MEDIUM 2026-08-31) — bare `Write-Host` rides
the information stream, so any script reporting through it shows nothing at
all through PTK unless redirected; that is silent loss of ubiquitous output,
not just of opt-in diagnostics. `-Verbose` and `-InformationAction Continue`
content is likewise absent from both the shaped response and the immutable
recovery artifact, so none of it is recoverable after the fact. Errors and
warnings are unaffected and remain first-class.
**Status**: CONFIRMED in current source (2026-08-31, head `15acc415`) — not
fixed-since-rc.1. Design ruling requested from the owner; no repair without
it.
**Branch**: — (default-branch mode)
**Commit**: observed during the issue #30 acceptance run at repo head `b39c3ae7`;
source-confirmed at `15acc415`

## Current-source confirmation (2026-08-31)

- `server/PtkMcpServer/RunspaceHost.cs:1701-1703` — exactly three streams are
  drained: output, error, warning. `:3914` — the passive capture attaches
  `ps.Streams.Error` and `ps.Streams.Warning` only.
- Zero references to `VerboseRecord`, `InformationRecord`, `Write-Verbose`,
  or `Write-Information` anywhere in `server/PtkMcpServer` or its tests: the
  verbose, information, and progress collections are never read by any code
  path, shaped or captured.
- Live through the installed build: `Write-Host 'ptk-proof-host-visible';
  'pipeline_output_here'` returned only `pipeline_output_here` — the
  `Write-Host` line vanished. (The earlier state-file note that `Write-Host`
  "survives" applies only under an explicit `*>&1` merge redirect.)

## Evidence

Observed live on `ASHBIAMWEB1` (installed PTK `0.3.0-rc.1`) during the issue
#30 warm on-prem remoting acceptance, session `exch`:

```
Write-Warning 'ptk-proof-warning'; Write-Verbose 'ptk-proof-verbose' -Verbose;
Write-Information 'ptk-proof-information' -InformationAction Continue;
Write-Progress -Activity 'ptk-proof-progress' -PercentComplete 50;
'synthetic_streams_done'
```

- Shaped response: output line plus `[warnings] ptk-proof-warning` only.
- The same call's `ptk_output` recovery artifact (`complete=true`, 55 bytes,
  `provenance=powershell_objects`) carries the identical content — the
  verbose, information, and progress records are not retained anywhere.
- A remoting proxy call with `-Verbose` (`Get-ExchangeServer -Verbose`
  through an `Import-PSSession` proxy) likewise surfaced no verbose records;
  with the synthetic result above, the loss is attributable to capture, not
  to the proxy.

Issue #30 step 5 requires recording the observed treatment of progress,
verbose, and information and raising a finding for any non-first-class
result rather than silently accepting it. Treatment observed: errors
first-class (uncaught remote error rendered with full message, call marked
failed, explicitly not retried), warnings first-class, progress dropped,
verbose dropped, information dropped.

## Assessment

Dropping progress is defensible by design for a token compressor: progress
records are transient UI. Verbose and information differ: the caller opted
in per call, the records can carry the only diagnostic trail of a remote
operation, and "recoverable via `ptk_output`" is the product's own stated
answer to lossy shaping — an answer that does not currently hold for these
two streams. Whether the right contract is "render in shaped output",
"retain only in the recovery artifact", or "drop and document" is a design
ruling, not a repair to make silently.

## Next step

Reproduction complete: current source drops the same three streams, and the
`Write-Host` case raises the stakes from lost diagnostics to lost ordinary
output. The design question is with the owner: render verbose/information
(and Write-Host) first-class like warnings, retain them only in the recovery
artifact, or document the drop as the capture contract. Progress-dropping is
recommended to stay as-is by design. No code change until the owner rules.
