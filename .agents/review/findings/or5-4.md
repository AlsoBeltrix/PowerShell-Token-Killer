# or5-4: background job capabilities are never released, capping a session at 64 background jobs

**Severity**: MEDIUM — 64 completed background jobs permanently wedge new
background starts (and cross-alias) until a full reset destroys the warm
runspace the tool exists to preserve.
**Status**: Verified
**Branch**: `feature/mcp-resilience-r1` (worktree `.claude/worktrees/mcp-resilience-r1`)
**Commit**: `a0a034f`; reviewed range `8ecf4d1..a0a034f`

## Evidence
`WorkerPrivateHostRuntime.ExecuteBackgroundAsync` gated on the sum of all
aliases' `JobCapabilities.Count >= MaximumOutstandingPrivateRequests`
(64); entries were inserted on start and only ever removed by `Clear()` on
shutdown/replacement/close — never on job terminal.

## Predicted observable failure
A long-lived session completes 64 background jobs; the 65th background
invoke returns SessionBusy/SessionUnavailable forever, including on other
aliases, until the user resets and loses warm state.

## What
Per-alias maps split into `OutstandingJobs` (gated, summed across aliases)
and `CompletedJobs` (per-alias, capped at 64 with oldest-insertion
eviction by monotonic job ID). The outstanding entry is reserved under
the gate BEFORE the prepared commit write and removed on refused/failed
start, so a fast job's terminal can never precede the insert. The worker
event bridge reports each validated job terminal to the runtime
(`JobTerminalObserved`), which moves the entry from Outstanding to
Completed. `ExecuteJobOperationAsync` authorizes against either map;
evicted completed jobs are `JobCapabilityInvalid`. Replacement, close,
shutdown, and the or5-1 fault path clear both maps.

## Files changed
`WorkerPrivateHostRuntime.cs` (map split, reserve-before-commit, release),
`PrivateHostWorkerEventBridge.cs` (`JobTerminalObserved` hook),
`WorkerPrivateHostRuntimeTests.cs` (fake echoes prepared job ID, terminal
helpers, two new tests).
