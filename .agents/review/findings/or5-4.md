# or5-4: background job capabilities are never released, capping a session at 64 background jobs

**Severity**: MEDIUM — 64 completed background jobs permanently wedge new
background starts (and cross-alias) until a full reset destroys the warm
runspace the tool exists to preserve.
**Status**: Open
**Branch**: `feature/mcp-resilience-r1` (worktree `.claude/worktrees/mcp-resilience-r1`)
**Commit**: —

## Evidence
`WorkerPrivateHostRuntime.ExecuteBackgroundAsync` gates on the sum of all
aliases' `JobCapabilities.Count >= MaximumOutstandingPrivateRequests`
(64); entries are inserted on start and only ever removed by `Clear()` on
shutdown/replacement/close — never on job terminal.

## Predicted observable failure
A long-lived session completes 64 background jobs; the 65th background
invoke returns SessionBusy/SessionUnavailable forever, including on other
aliases, until the user resets and loses warm state.

## What
Separate the budgets: release the outstanding entry when the job's
terminal event arrives through `PrivateHostWorkerEventBridge` and gate new
starts on genuinely outstanding count; retain completed-job capabilities
(needed for post-completion `ptk_job output`) in a separate per-alias
structure bounded independently.

## Guard proof
New test: after the 64th background start, the 65th is refused; a job
terminal for one entry frees exactly one slot and the next start succeeds.
