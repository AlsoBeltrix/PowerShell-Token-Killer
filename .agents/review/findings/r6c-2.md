## Predicted observable failure

- Open dispatched without declaring the alias first must be refused
  (SessionNotFound), never dispatched. Covered by
  `GuardianHostSupervisorTests.Public_session_open_declares_and_dispatches_a_dynamic_alias_with_a_null_worker`.
- The open request must carry `Worker = null`. Covered by the same test's
  `HasWorkerIdentity` assertion on the fake peer.
- Reopening an existing alias must fail `SessionBusy` and launch no worker;
  template open must fail `UnsupportedOperation`. Covered by
  `WorkerPrivateHostRuntimeTests.Open_creates_a_new_alias_worker_and_returns_its_identity`
  and `Public_session_open_with_a_template_is_refused_before_any_declaration`.
- Declared aliases must project Cold with null worker until the first grant,
  then bind the host-selected worker at generation 2. Covered by
  `FrozenDefaultSessionStateTests.Declared_dynamic_alias_projects_cold_until_its_first_grant_binds_a_worker`.
- The real apphost must open, invoke on, and close a dynamic session.
  Covered by
  `ProductionGuardianCompositionTests.Composition_opens_a_dynamic_session_on_the_real_private_host`.

## What

R6 sub-slice 4's dynamic-alias half: `ptk_session open` end-to-end per
`.agents/plans/mcp-resilience.md` and the mapped design in `.agents/state.md`.

## Approach

The guardian declares dynamic aliases into its per-alias declared state; the
supervisor dispatches open through the Host gate with a null-worker request;
the host rebuilds the binding and computes the shared digest so the guardian's
capability grant catches any divergence; the `RequestedOpen` lifecycle binds
the real host-selected worker before the operation result is validated.
