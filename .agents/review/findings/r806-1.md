## Evidence

`server/PtkMcpServer/Sessions/WorkerSupervisor.cs:489` — `StartedDetailCodes` maps **every** `descendants_unknown` to `OutcomeUnknown`, on the stated theory (remarks, lines 478–488) that close and reset raise that code only AFTER acting on the worker.

Reachable: a prior operation that leaves `slot.ContainmentEmpty` unconfirmed on a surviving slot (e.g. a start failure with `DescendantsUnknown` containment, lines 607–613), followed by a fresh `ptk_reset` or close, which stops at the preflight having touched nothing.

## Predicted observable failure

After containment is left unconfirmed by a *previous* operation, the next `ptk_reset`/`ptk_session close` that stops at preflight returns disposition `outcome_unknown`, `executed=true`, and text "The operation had already begun; do not assume it did nothing." — all false for the current operation, which never started and is safe to resubmit. A client following the trusted verdict declines a retry it could safely make.

## What

The classifier infers execution stage from the detail code alone, but `descendants_unknown` is emitted at two different stages: post-action (CloseSlotAsync, the case the remarks describe) and preflight refusal (nothing touched). This is the o53 lesson recurring at a third site: PTK stating a false verdict in the channel a client is told to trust. Reviewer's suggested direction: carry the stage on the exception (typed disposition or distinct detail codes for preflight vs post-action), never infer it from a shared string.
