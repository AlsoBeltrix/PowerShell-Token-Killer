# s5-1: unexpected worker death auto-recovers on the alias's next generation

**Severity**: HIGH — until this landed, nothing detected an unexpected
worker exit at all: operations kept dispatching at a dead process, and the
loss surfaced only as repeated outcome_unknown.
**Status**: Verified
**Branch**: `feature/mcp-resilience-r1` (worktree `.claude/worktrees/mcp-resilience-r1`)
**Commit**: `d9f9c2b`; repairs `0d6ffe1`, `0a84e45`, `49b9602`

## Evidence
`WorkerPrivateHostRuntime` had no worker-death detection: no watch on
`IWorkerProcessClient.Fatal`, no slot cleanup, no relaunch. The
`SessionRecoveryStateMachine` integration-order finding (or5-5) directed
the thinnest live path first.

## Predicted observable failure
A worker exits unexpectedly: the alias must detect it, confirm containment,
and relaunch at the next generation with an `AutomaticRecovery` lifecycle
and `warm_state_lost=true` — while a second alias keeps its PID,
generation, and operations; a crash loop must stop (fault only that
alias) instead of relaunching forever; operations during the gap must get
a retryable WorkerLost, not a terminal-looking SessionFaulted.

## What
Per alias: a watch on `slot.Process.Fatal` (armed at Ready and at every
slot assignment); single-flight arbitration through the existing
`BeginReplacement` fence plus a watcher slot-identity check (stale watches
back off); containment confirmation via slot disposal; relaunch through
the existing capability-grant slot path with an `AutomaticRecovery`
lifecycle; a consecutive-death counter with stability-window reset that
faults the alias (CircuitTransition) on the third consecutive death;
`ReplacingAutomatically` so gap operations get WorkerLost; counter clears
on manual replacement, reopen, and close completion.

## Guard proof
Eleven rig tests cover: recovery with a second alias intact, crash-loop
fault, stale-watcher backoff, init-time death recovery (non-vacuous,
deterministic wait), WorkerLost in a gated gap, manual-reset counter
clear, spaced-deaths stability reset, and the failed-close reopen counter
clear. Mutations verified red-to-green: both slot fences jointly removed,
loop-stop removed, relaunch removed, in-loop init arming restored
(deterministic timeout), reopen counter clear removed. Full battery
2,594/2,594.
